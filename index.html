The Invitation

At exactly midnight, an old envelope appeared beneath your door. There was no stamp, no address and no name.

Inside, you found a photograph of a house. On its back, four names had been written in red ink: Anna, Elisa, Riya and Naziha.

Beneath them, a final sentence: "The door will open on October 31st. But not everyone who enters will leave unchanged."
