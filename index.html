THE HOUSE THAT REMEMBERS

CHAPTER I · THE INVITATION

At 23:47, an old envelope appears outside your door. There is no stamp. No return address. Only your name, written in black ink. Inside, you find a photograph of a house. On the back, someone has written: "You have already been invited. You just haven't accepted yet."

CHAPTER II · THE HOUSE

The photograph begins to change. A dark figure appears behind the window. Your phone lights up. A message: "Don't let it see you."

CHAPTER III · THE LAST DOOR

You find yourself inside the house. There is a locked door. On it, a message: "To leave, you must answer what I ask. Fail, and I will remember your face."
