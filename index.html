THE STORY OF RUE RENOZ 26

In 1923, a lone passenger arrived carrying a brass key marked with the number 26. He walked into the fog of Rue Renoz and vanished into the shadows of the basement.

Witnesses swore that before he disappeared, he opened a velvet sack and left behind a single warning message on the wall written in red ink: "The third door stays locked until midnight."
